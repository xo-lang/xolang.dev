### create_certificate {#create_certificate}

```xo
fn create_certificate(r: Rand, t: Template, public_key: crypto.PublicKey, parent: Certificate?, key: crypto.PrivateKey) -> Certificate ! CertErr uses rand
```

Go's CreateCertificate: a certificate of t for public_key, issued by
parent and signed with parent's private key, key; self-signed when
parent is None (the issuer is t's subject and key the matching private
key). The signature algorithm is Go's for the key: SHA256-RSA,
ECDSA-SHA256, -SHA384 or -SHA512 by curve, or Ed25519. Err with Go's
text for a name that is not IA5, a max_path_len on a non-CA, a key that
is not parent's, or a P-224 key (Go cannot sign with one).

[Source: `std/x509.xo:184`](https://github.com/xo-lang/xo/blob/main/std/x509.xo#L184)
