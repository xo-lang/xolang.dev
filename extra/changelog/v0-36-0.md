### Idle connections against Go

Server memory for each idle keep-alive connection (RssAnon on the linux/arm64 offload host, bench/perf/idleconn; [decision 0449](https://github.com/xo-lang/xo/blob/main/knowledgebase/decisions/0449-idle-connection-memory.md)). Before this release an idle connection held a task on its own fiber stack: at 1,000 and 5,000 connections Xo used 38 and 17 KB for HTTP/1.1 against Go's 27 and 21 KB, and 127 and 91 KB for HTTP/2 over TLS against Go's 53 and 44 KB ([lines 15 to 20](https://github.com/xo-lang/xo/blob/main/knowledgebase/decisions/0449-idle-connection-memory.md#L15-L20)). Each stack also cost two memory mappings, so Linux's default `vm.max_map_count` capped a server at about 32,000 idle connections ([lines 35 to 38](https://github.com/xo-lang/xo/blob/main/knowledgebase/decisions/0449-idle-connection-memory.md#L35-L38)). With idle connections parked:

- **HTTP/1.1:** 40,000 idle connections take 255 MB, 6.4 KB each, in 293 mappings ([lines 202 to 205](https://github.com/xo-lang/xo/blob/main/knowledgebase/decisions/0449-idle-connection-memory.md#L202-L205)).
- **HTTP/2 over TLS:** 40,000 idle connections take 918,892 kB, about 23 KB each, in 470 mappings, against Go's 44 to 53 KB ([lines 224 to 228](https://github.com/xo-lang/xo/blob/main/knowledgebase/decisions/0449-idle-connection-memory.md#L224-L228)).
- **HTTP/1.1 over TLS:** 40,000 idle connections take 752,584 kB, about 18.8 KB each ([lines 259 to 261](https://github.com/xo-lang/xo/blob/main/knowledgebase/decisions/0449-idle-connection-memory.md#L259-L261)); Go was not measured in this mode.

The comparison has limits. Go's figures are from 1,000 and 5,000 connections, not 40,000. The TLS engine is about 19 KB of HTTP/2's 23 KB, and a parked connection keeps its 32 KB read buffer and its write buffers ([lines 228 to 232](https://github.com/xo-lang/xo/blob/main/knowledgebase/decisions/0449-idle-connection-memory.md#L228-L232)). Where Xo still holds more is slow keep-alive traffic: a plain HTTP/1.1 connection waits 20 ms on its own stack before it parks, which added 14 to 23 MB at 2,000 and 10,000 connections with 10 to 30 ms between requests ([lines 295 to 306](https://github.com/xo-lang/xo/blob/main/knowledgebase/decisions/0449-idle-connection-memory.md#L295-L306)); 0.36.0 also parks such a connection at once after its first linger times out, until it is busy again ([lines 313 to 318](https://github.com/xo-lang/xo/blob/main/knowledgebase/decisions/0449-idle-connection-memory.md#L313-L318)).
