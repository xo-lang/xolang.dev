Self-hosted web fonts (served from xolang.dev, not from Google's hosts):

- space-grotesk-latin.woff2: Space Grotesk (variable, weights 300 to 700),
  by Florian Karsten, from fonts.gstatic.com spacegrotesk v22, Latin subset.
- jetbrains-mono-latin.woff2: JetBrains Mono (variable, weights 100 to 800),
  by JetBrains, from fonts.gstatic.com jetbrainsmono v24, Latin subset.

Both are under the SIL Open Font License 1.1
(https://openfontlicense.org), which permits bundling and redistribution
with a website. The @font-face rules are at the top of assets/css/main.css.
